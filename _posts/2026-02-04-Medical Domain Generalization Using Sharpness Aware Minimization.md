<h5>
    Hyunjyung Han - MICV, AI, Yonsei University
</h5>

Domain generalization (DG) is a fundamental and unresolved challenge in real-world medical AI. Despite strong in-domain performance, models trained on data from limited institutions often fail when deployed in unseen clinical environments. In this presentation, we revisit the DG problem from an optimization perspective and pose a simple yet compelling question: can improving the geometry of the loss landscape lead to more robust medical imaging models? Motivated by recent successes in natural image analysis, an ongoing research exploring Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM) as a data-agnostic approach to medical DG is introduced.
