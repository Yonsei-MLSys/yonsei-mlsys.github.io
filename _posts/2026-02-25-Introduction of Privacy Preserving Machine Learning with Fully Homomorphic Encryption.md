The deployment of machine learning in sensitive domains necessitates Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning (PPML) to ensure strict data confidentiality. Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) provides a foundation for PPML by enabling arbitrary computations directly on encrypted data without requiring decryption. To overcome the inherent programming and performance challenges of FHE, we present HECATE, an optimizing compiler framework. HECATE translates standard deep learning models into efficient FHE programs, bridging the gap between theoretical cryptography and practical, secure AI inference.
