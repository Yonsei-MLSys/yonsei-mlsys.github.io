title: "Research Trends in Video Motion Control and Training-Free Methods on Video Diffusion Transformers"
---

<h5>
    Kyobin Choo - MICV Lab, CS, Yonsei University
</h5>

This talk presents an overview of research on motion-controllable video generation, including motion transfer and user-specified motion control.
In particular, it focuses on user-specified motion control and examines how the shift in backbone architecture from diffusion U-Nets to diffusion transformers (DiTs) affects the applicability of existing training-free methods. We analyze to what extent these U-Net–based approaches remain effective in DiTs, and discuss the underlying reasons for their limitations.
Finally, we introduce recent work by Kyobin Choo, which proposes a novel training-free framework for enabling user-specified motion control in DiT-based video generation.
