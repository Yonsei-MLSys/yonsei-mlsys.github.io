This presentation covers recent work on DeepSeek, RLVR, and rollout generation. It begins with the DeepSeek series and recent advances in reasoning language models, and then explains the core idea of reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR), where models are trained using outcome-based rewards rather than direct supervision of intermediate reasoning steps. The second part focuses on rollout generation, an essential but often costly component of reasoning-oriented reinforcement learning. In particular, it discusses why rollouts matter, why they often become a major computational bottleneck, and how recent studies seek to improve both their efficiency and diversity. Based on these topics, the presentation concludes by introducing my next research direction.
