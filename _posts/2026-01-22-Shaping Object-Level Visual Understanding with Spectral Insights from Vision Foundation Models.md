This presentation provides a comprehensive retrospective of my three-year Master’s journey. My primary research interest has centered on leveraging Vision Foundation Models for enhanced visual understanding and medical imaging tasks. Specifically, I have investigated methods to adapt large-scale models to specific domains, aiming to bridge the gap between general vision capabilities and specialized medical applications. Beyond my research, I actively engaged in diverse academic activities—ranging from collaborative projects to technical seminars—which served to broaden my research perspective and technical horizon. While my academic timeline extended to three years following a transition in my degree track, this period was instrumental in solidifying my research capabilities and defining my future direction. As I prepare to pursue my doctoral studies in the US, I will summarize the key research milestones and professional experiences that have prepared me for this next chapter.
