Near-Data Processing (NDP) is a vital solution for overcoming data movement bottlenecks in AI systems, but its adoption requires a practical and synergistic design approach. My research focuses on building cost-effective NDP systems by selectively offloading tasks with low arithmetic intensity and optimizing host-device cooperation to maximize overall system potential. These principles are demonstrated through Piccolo (HPCA ’25), which implements NDP in DRAM, and HILOS (ASPLOS ’26), which extends this paradigm to SSDs. By prioritizing hardware practicality and FPGA-based verification, these works showcase a viable and scalable path toward next-generation AI acceleration in real-world computing environments.
